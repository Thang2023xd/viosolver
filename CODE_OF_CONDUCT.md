# Code of Conduct

This project is for respectful, lawful collaboration on authorized QA work.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, reviews, and discussions.
- Share clear reproduction steps and redact credentials, staff keys, and personal data.
- Respect the authorization limits and third-party service terms described in `TERMS.md`.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or deliberate disruption of project work.
- Sharing another person's credentials, private data, or unredacted screenshots.
- Using the project to access or interfere with systems without authorization.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed and handled at the owner's discretion; actions may include a warning, removal from project access, or reporting abuse to the relevant service.
